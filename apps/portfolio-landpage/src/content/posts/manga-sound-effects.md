If you have scrolled around this site, you have seen big inky Japanese letters stamped across the cards. They are
not captions. They are manga sound effects, and they do what they do in a comic: make a quiet panel feel loud.

## What are they?

Japanese comics draw sound straight into the art. These words are called giongo (擬音語, sounds) and gitaigo (擬態語,
feelings and motion), and they are almost always written in katakana, the angular script used for loud, foreign, or
emphatic words.

The little ッ at the end of many of them is a small "tsu". It is not pronounced; it cuts the sound off sharply, like
an exclamation point you can hear. A long dash (ー) stretches the vowel out.

Everything below is decoration, so it is hidden from screen readers. This post is the transcript.

## Around the site

- **ドン! (don!)** on the intro splash. "BOOM!" The classic dramatic-entrance beat, made famous by One Piece.
- **ゴゴゴ (gogogo)** in the hero. A low, menacing rumble that builds tension. If you have read JoJo's Bizarre
  Adventure, you can already hear it.
- **バーン (bān)** in the contact section. "BANG!" or "slam!", the sound of a big reveal.
- **ガーン (gān)** on the 404 page. The gong of shock and dismay: "oh no...".
- **完 (kan)** in the footer. Not a sound at all. It means "complete" and is printed on the last page of a manga, like
  "The End".

## On the projects

Each project gets the noise it would make if it were a manga character.

- **AI gateway, シュッ (shu')**: a swift "whoosh". Requests go in and get routed out fast.
- **Graph Weave, ドン (don)**: a heavy "boom". Graphs are big, and they land with weight.
- **Mac OCR, カリッ (kari')**: a crisp "scritch", like a pen scratching or a quick crunch. Text gets scraped off the
  screen.
- **Posturify, ゾクッ (zoku')**: a shiver down your spine. That is exactly how it feels when the app catches you
  slouching.
- **Wealth management, ゴゴゴ (gogogo)**: the menacing rumble again. Power building up, slowly.
- **Web crawler, ザワ (zawa)**: an uneasy murmur, the stir of a crowd. Kaiji fans know it as "zawa zawa".

## Off the clock

- **Bass guitar, ブン (bun)**: a deep "vrmm", a string humming low.
- **Cycling, シャー (shā)**: the hiss and whirr of wheels on the road.
- **Running, タッ (ta')**: a quick footstep, the moment you push off.
- **Manga, ドン (don)**: what else would it be?
- **Legend of Zelda, ヤッ (ya')**: "Hyah!", Link's battle shout.
- **Gamification, ピコ (piko)**: an 8-bit "bleep".
- **Learning languages, ペラ (pera)**: half of ペラペラ (perapera), which means speaking a language fluently. It is
  the goal, written as a sound.
- **Chess, カチ (kachi)**: the "click" of a piece set down, or the chess clock being slapped.
- **Math, ピン (pin)**: "ping!", the moment an idea lands. ピンとくる (pin to kuru) is how you say "it clicked".
- **Music, ジャン (jan)**: "ta-da!", a strummed chord and a flourish.

## Read them out loud

The best way to learn these is to say them, the louder the better. Next time a deploy goes green, try a ドン!
